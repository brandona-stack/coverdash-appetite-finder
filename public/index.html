    How to read this

    Estimated premium

    The estimate comes from real quotes we've received for similar businesses. For each carrier, the tool finds quotes for the same policy type and class whose revenue is between half and double what you entered. For Workers' Comp it compares payroll instead. It adjusts those quotes to your number and shows the median, with the middle 50% range underneath.

    If the carrier hasn't quoted enough in this exact class and state, the estimate broadens to all states, then to the wider NAICS group (for example, 2382 instead of 238220). When that happens, the row tells you which level it used.

    For Workers' Comp, the search stays in the same state and widens the class first, because WC rates depend so much on the state.

    When we tested this against past quotes, a typical estimate was within about 25% of the actual price. For GL, BOP, Professional and Cyber, about two out of three estimates were within ±50%. Workers' Comp is looser: about half were within ±50%, because WC class codes don't line up neatly with NAICS. Umbrella, Property and Inland Marine don't have enough data to be reliable. Treat the estimate as a ballpark to set expectations. It isn't a quote.

    Subcontracted work

    Most of the quotes behind these estimates are for accounts with little or no subcontracted work. Expect real quotes on high-sub accounts to come in above the estimate, especially for GL.

    Above 25% sub, standard carriers are removed: The Hartford, Chubb, Acuity, Travelers, Hiscox, CNA, biBerk, Nationwide, Employers, Guard, Hanover, Amtrust, Three by Berkshire, Markel and Great American. Coterie considers up to 50%. The removed carriers are listed under the table.

    Can write this vs. tough fit

    A carrier is a tough fit when it mostly refers or declines this class, or when the account is more than twice the size of anything it has quoted here. Every other carrier that has quoted this class is listed under Can write this. The big range at the top runs from the cheapest carrier's typical estimate to the priciest carrier's.

    Track record

    Bound counts the businesses we've bound with that carrier. Bind rate is bound ÷ businesses that carrier priced. Carriers the online rater quotes automatically (Coterie, Hiscox, Chubb) quote almost everyone, so their bind rates look lower.
